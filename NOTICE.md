## Source data

The procurement data used in this project is published by the **Bureau of
Public Procurement (BPP), Federal Republic of Nigeria**, through the Nigeria
Open Contracting Portal (NOCOPO, http://nocopo.bpp.gov.ng). The BPP publishes it
under the **ODC Public Domain Dedication and Licence (ODC-PDDL)**.

The raw data is not included in this repository. See `data/README.md` for the
source inventory, checksum and acquisition notes.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent portfolio analysis. It is not an official BPP
publication and is not endorsed by the BPP or any procuring entity. Findings
describe patterns in published data. They do not allege fraud, corruption,
misconduct or causation, and they are not a substitute for an official audit.
